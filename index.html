Welcome!

I am a PhD candidate in Economics at the European University Institute (EUI). I work on labour economics, with a focus on wage bargaining and firm competition. My secondary field is industrial policy.

I am on the 2026–2027 academic job market. My Job Market Paper studies how worker bargaining power varies with labour market tightness.

My supervisors are Russell Cooper and Giacomo Calzolari .
